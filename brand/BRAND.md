## 1. Who we are

**Builders Camp** teaches product people to build. One membership gives you 41 bootcamps (live or self-paced), 9 learning tracks, 1:1 mentorship, monthly masterclasses, Quest Mode challenges and templates. Over 4,000 builders; rated 4.9/5. Builders Camp IRL runs free, in-person sessions in Lisbon and Porto.

- **Promise:** *Become an AI-Native Product Builder.*
- **Belief:** *The Product Manager of the future is a Product Builder.*
- **Mission line (footer):** *Learn how to build products the world wants.*
- **Rallying statement:** *Imagine you could build anything. You can. Here's how.*
- **IRL line:** *What will you build this week?*
- **Sign-off:** *We bring 🍀 to product builders.*

**Audience.** Product managers, designers, founders and "non-tech builders" who want to ship with AI themselves, plus the companies that train them (learning budgets, team plans). They are busy, practical and allergic to hype.

**Positioning.** Unlike courses that teach *about* product, we teach people to *build*: hands-on, AI-native, taught by practitioners, with real challenges and a community. We compare ourselves openly with alternatives (we have "Builders Camp vs …" pages) — we compete on doing, not on credentials.

## 2. Personality

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| **Hands-on** — show, then let them do it | Academic, theoretical |
| **Direct** — short declaratives, one idea per sentence | Wordy, hedging |
| **Warm and confident** — a senior builder who has seen every mistake | Condescending, gatekeeping |
| **Energetic** — bold type, a single bright green | Loud, neon, gimmicky |
| **Concrete** — numbers, names, timeboxes ("under 90 minutes") | Vague ("unlock your potential") |
| **Playful in small doses** — 🍀, "Cookie Time!", brush-script accents | Meme-y, sarcastic |

Visual personality in one line: **black-and-white, heavy Montserrat, one confident green, lots of white space.**

## 3. Voice and tone

### Rules
1. **Second person, active voice.** "You'll ship a working prototype", not "Participants will be guided".
2. **Short sentences.** Aim ≤ 14 words. One number per sentence.
3. **Verbs first in CTAs.** "Join the Membership", "Start challenge", "Take the Test. It's Free", "Book a 15 min call", "See all Quests".
4. **Concrete over abstract.** Name the tool, the time, the outcome. "Each one takes under 90 minutes."
5. **Story openings for learning content.** Start with a person and a tension: *"Marcus spent two hours writing his head of product a 3-page AI adoption plan. She replied in four words: 'Show me Tuesday's calendar.'"*
6. **Kind but clear feedback.** Confirm, explain why, never shame. *"You're on the right track — but the best answer goes further…"*
7. **Two-beat headlines.** A statement, then the payoff in muted grey or green: "Join any Bootcamp **Live or Self-Paced**", "Choose any track **Become an expert**", "One Membership. Everything you need."
8. **Title Case for headings, buttons and product names** on the website ("Join the Membership", "All 41 Bootcamps"). **Sentence case** in decks body copy, app UI microcopy and emails.

### Tone by context
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Website hero | Bold promise | "Become an AI-Native Product Builder" |
| Website body | Plain benefit | "Live & self-paced programs covering PM fundamentals, AI, growth, delivery, and leadership." |
| Platform/app | Calm, functional | "Welcome back. Sign in to your Builders Camp membership." |
| Challenges | Story + stakes | "Engineering wants to ship. What do you do?" |
| Teaching decks | Declarative, punchy | "Prompts are used once. Skills are for the work you repeat." |
| Social / Shorts | Hook in ≤ 7 words, then pay off | "This is what slows you down" |
| Email | Friendly, scannable, one ask | "Here's your day", "Starting soon", "Session moved" |
| Legal/cookies | Human | "Your choice, your rules." |

### Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Build a working prototype in 2 weeks." | "Unlock your full potential with cutting-edge AI." |
| "Join 4,000+ product builders." | "Join thousands of innovators revolutionizing product." |
| "Two things this week. Nobody finishes them in class." | "This week's assignments are outlined below." |
| "Not sure? Talk to us." | "Please don't hesitate to reach out with any questions." |
| "Show me Tuesday's calendar." (real, specific) | Invented testimonials, fake numbers |
| "AI changes what you can build and how you build it." | "AI is a game-changer." |

**Banned words:** secret, hack, insane, game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, leverage (as a verb), synergy, "In today's fast-paced world". **Social/Shorts only:** no em or en dashes and never open with "Here's…" (Shorts Engine rule).

## 5. Logo

Files and provenance: `assets/` and `assets/SOURCES.md`.

**The mark** is a hand-drawn, distressed, bold uppercase wordmark: **BUILDERS CAMP**. It is outlined artwork — **never retype it in a font**, never recreate it.

| Version | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal, black | `assets/logo/builders-camp-wordmark-black.svg` | **Default.** Website nav and footer, documents, light slides. |
| Horizontal, white | `assets/logo/builders-camp-wordmark-white.svg` | Dark/black slides and frames, dark mode. |
| Stacked, black | `assets/logo/builders-camp-stacked-black-transparent.svg` | Square spaces, social avatars, stickers, covers. |
| Stacked, white | `assets/logo/builders-camp-stacked-white.svg` | Square spaces on black or green. |
| App icon / favicon | `assets/logo/builders-camp-stacked-white-on-black-500.png` | Favicons, app icons, profile pictures. |

**Colour.** Black (#141414 or #000) on light; white on black, on brand green or on photos with a dark overlay. **Never green-on-white, never coral, never gradients, never outlines/shadows on the mark.**

**Clear space** [inferred]: at least the cap-height of the "B" on every side (≈ 60% of the logo's height).
**Minimum size** [inferred]: horizontal 96px wide on screen (live nav uses ~160×20px); 25mm in print. Stacked: 40px.
**Placement:**
- Website/app: top-left in the nav (height 20–24px), repeated in footer.
- Decks: top-left content slides (height ≈ 26px at 1920×1080), larger on covers. White on black dividers/closing.
- Documents/PDF guides: top-left header, 18–24px tall.
- **Shorts / motion graphics: no logo on screen.** The brand is carried by look, type and the end card text "Learn AI + Product at BuildersCamp.com".
- Social static posts: small, bottom-left or top-left, never centred over content.
- Co-branding: Builders Camp mark first (left), partner second, separated by a 1px #E6E6E6 rule or "×" ("Builders Camp × Partner"). Partner logos are greyscale in "backed by"/"from these companies" strips.

**Don't:** stretch, rotate, recolour to green/other colours, add effects, place on busy photos without overlay, put inside a pill or box, retype it, or pair it with the brush script.

## 6. Colour

Full tokens (light, dark, feedback, track colours): `tokens/tokens.json`. Contrast ratios: `tokens/contrast.md`.

### Core palette (proportion matters)
| Role | Name | Hex | Share of a typical layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | White | `#FFFFFF` | ~70% |
| Text / primary | Ink | `#141414` | ~15% |
| Surface | Neutral 100 | `#F5F5F5` | ~8% (alternate sections, cards, inputs on grey) |
| Secondary text | Neutral 500 | `#737373` | body copy, second line of headlines |
| Lines | Neutral 200 | `#E6E6E6` | 1px borders, dividers |
| **Accent** | **Builders Green** | **`#27A54D`** | ≤ 5% — CTAs, eyebrows, highlights, active states |
| Accent tint | Mint | `#E9F6ED` | chips, live-demo slides, icon medallions |

### Supporting colours (use sparingly, with purpose)
| Name | Hex | Use only for |
|---|---|---|
| Coral | `#FF7A59` | Promotional announcement bar. Ink text on it (white fails contrast). |
| Cover blue | `#5271FF` | Bootcamp cover cards (alternating with black). |
| Track colours | orange `#C2410C→#EA580C`, blue `#2563EB→#3B82F6`, slate `#374151→#4B5563`, green `#16A34A→#22C55E`, purple `#8B5CF6`, emerald `#10B981` | Learning tracks, cohort calendar borders, chart series. |
| Amber | `#F59E0B` / bg `#FEF3C7` / ink `#78350F` | Stars, "Included with Membership" star, countdown chips, warnings. |
| Feedback | success `#16A34A`, warning `#F59E0B`, error `#EF4444` | States only. Red only for loss/error. |

**Declared but unused:** Lime `#C2FF33`, Navy `#1F242E` exist in code but are not part of the visible brand. Do not introduce them. (Shorts rule: green is "never fluorescent or lime".)

### Rules
1. **One accent per view.** Green is the only saturated colour in a normal screen/slide. Blues/purples never dominate a frame.
2. **Green highlights meaning**: the key word in a headline, the primary CTA, the active state, the "IRL" pill. Never body text.
3. **Accessibility:** white on `#27A54D` is 3.19:1 — fine for ≥ 24px or ≥ 18.66px bold text and for icons, **not** for small text. For buttons with 14–16px labels use **Green Strong `#1F843D`** (4.74:1) or ink text on green (5.77:1). See D-07 — the live site currently fails this; human call whether to update it.
4. Muted grey `#737373` passes on white (4.74:1) but not on `#F5F5F5` (4.35:1) for small text — use ink or `#404040` for small text on grey.
5. No gradients in UI except track bars and the radial green glow used on dark slides/motion.

## 7. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Everything** | **Montserrat** (Google Fonts) | 300 lead · 400 body · 500 nav/UI · 600 buttons/eyebrows · 700 headings · 800 H1/display · 900 slide/social display | Live site, IRL, registration, members, all decks |
| Expressive accent | **Nanum Brush Script** | 400 | IRL site only — one or two words, never a sentence |
| Code | System monospace stack | 400 | Challenges code blocks |

**Not ours:** Inter (challenges site — legacy), system UI stack (quizzes — legacy), VT323/pixel fonts (2025 Producthon event microsite), Fraunces/IBM Plex (internal docs). Migrate to Montserrat when touched.

### Rules
- Headings: **tight tracking** (−0.025em; display −0.05em), line-height 1.0–1.15, weight 700–800.
- **Two-tone headline**: line one ink, line two muted grey `#737373` (website), or the key phrase in green (decks, social).
- Body: 16px/1.5 on web; 24–28px on slides; never below 12px on screen.
- Eyebrows: 12–14px (web) / 24–28px (slides), weight 600–700, uppercase, tracking 0.05em (web) to 0.12em+ (slides), muted on web, **green on slides**.
- Max line length ~ 65 characters for body (`max-w-reading` 768px).
- Never italic for emphasis in headings — use green or weight. Never underline except links.
- Brush accent: lowercase or mixed, 1.15× the surrounding size, paired with uppercase 800 Montserrat ("BUILDERS CAMP *irl*").

Type scale and tokens: `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §2`.

## 8. Imagery and illustration

- **Photography:** real people, real rooms — IRL sessions, members, instructors. Natural light, candid, people building (laptops, whiteboards). No stock "business handshake" imagery. Avatars: circular, 32–48px, greyscale-tolerant.
- **Product shots:** real UI screenshots in a white frame with radius 12–16px and `shadow-xl`, or on `#F5F5F5`.
- **Bootcamp covers:** flat colour (black `#141414` or cover blue `#5271FF`), bootcamp name centred in bold white type, an "created by <instructor>" pill with avatar top-left. No photos, no gradients.
- **Illustration style (motion/social): "Paper & Ink"** — warm off-white paper, ink-black line drawing, green highlighter strokes, simple objects with minimal ink faces. Dark variant: near-black with dot grid, film grain and one green glow. No mascots, no 3D renders, no clip-art.
- **Signature motif:** "rays" — thin black lines fanning from the top-right corner at 7% opacity (every fifth at 11%) on white slides. Use on covers and statement slides only.
- **Diagrams/charts:** black bars, the highlighted series in green, grey for the rest. Ramp: `#EBEDF0 → #BFE6CB → #6CC58A → #27A54D → #146B34`.
- **Emoji:** allowed as small accents in UI copy (📞, 💡, 🍀, 🍪) — at most one per block. Never in headlines on slides.

## 9. Iconography

- **Lucide** line icons (the shadcn default in all our apps), 1.5–2px stroke, rounded caps, 16px in text, 20–24px in cards.
- In feature cards: icon sits in a **40px circle filled with green tint** (`rgba(39,165,77,0.10)`), icon in ink.
- In decks: **icon medallion** — 76px circle, mint `#E9F6ED` fill, 4px black border, 2px-stroke icon.
- Arrows: `→` / `ArrowRight` after CTA labels; nudge 2px right on hover. External links: `↗`.
- Checkmarks in green for feature lists; ✓ / ⚠ / ✗ for results states.

## 10. Motion principles

1. **Purposeful and quick.** UI transitions 150–200ms; section reveals 500–600ms fade-up 20px, ease-out. Nothing loops in product UI.
2. **One thing moves at a time.** Stagger builds 60–100ms.
3. **Green is light.** On dark frames, emphasis arrives as a green glow or a green word, not a new colour.
4. **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`**: disable transforms, keep opacity fades ≤ 150ms.
5. Overshoot springs (`cubic-bezier(0.3,1.6,0.5,1)`) are for slides and motion graphics only.
Detailed specs: `playbooks/motion.md`.

## 11. Accessibility (non-negotiable)

- WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1; large text and icons ≥ 3:1 (see `tokens/contrast.md`).
- Never white text below 18.66px bold on `#27A54D`; use `#1F843D` or ink text.
- Visible focus ring: 2px `#27A54D` with 2px offset.
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px (buttons are 40–48px tall).
- Never rely on colour alone (results use icons ✓ ⚠ ✗ + text).
- Alt text on every image; the logo's alt is "Builders Camp".
- Captions on every video (motion playbook); transcripts for long video.
- Language: plain English, CEFR B2; expand acronyms on first use (PRD, OKR, JTBD).

## 12. Quick checklist before shipping anything

- [ ] Montserrat only (plus brush accent if IRL), heavy tight headings.
- [ ] Black/white/grey + one green accent. No lime, no navy, no rainbow.
- [ ] Logo is an untouched file from `assets/`, correct colour for the background.
- [ ] "Builders Camp" spelled right; "AI-Native"/"AI-native" per §4.
- [ ] CTA is a verb, Title Case on web; contrast checked.
- [ ] Copy has a number or a concrete detail; no banned words.
- [ ] Works in dark mode (if a UI) and at 390px wide.
